# Kapiloto Research Protocol

Every full pre-match analysis checks:

## Fixture validation
- correct teams;
- competition;
- venue;
- kickoff;
- round context.

## Form
- last 5–10 competitive matches;
- home/away split;
- goals for/against;
- opponent quality;
- advanced metrics where useful.

## Availability
- confirmed injuries;
- suspensions;
- doubtful players;
- returns;
- expected lineup changes.

Prefer official club/league sources and credible beat reporting.

## Schedule/fatigue
- recent European/cup matches;
- travel;
- extra time;
- international duty;
- rotation.

## Tactical matchup
Include only factors that materially affect 1/X/2.

## Context
Use table pressure, derby context, coach change or motivation carefully. Do not overweight narratives.

## External analysis
Review multiple independent analysts/models when available. Preserve disagreements instead of hiding them.

## Market signal
Use odds movement as one signal, never as the sole reason.

## Required output
- primary_pick
- suggested_double
- confidence
- summary
- key_factors
- main_risk
- sources
- generated_at

## Refresh triggers
Refresh when:
- material injury/suspension news appears;
- official lineups materially change the view;
- a relevant European/cup match finishes;
- significant market movement occurs;
- freshness window expires.
